Winter backend — UI kit v5 (layouts & fields pass · Fable)

Team feedback applied: warning is now Marigold, a muted gold whose 500-step chroma (S 62) sits between Mantis (S 50) and Flame (S 68) instead of the old amber's S 92 — compare them in the palette strip. Brand fonts: Public Sans (base) + Mulish (headings), via --brand-font / --font-head, with true tabular numerals on numeric columns. Tabs are redesigned around a snowcap: the active tab shares the body surface and wears a rounded Pacific cap on its top edge — no skew, no trapezoids, no drop shadows. The dark form header is a lifted indigo surface (gradient + frost highlight + lighter edge) so it separates cleanly from slate-900. List totals are real table-totals rows in both thead and tfoot — page sum with the grand total in parentheses, each directly under its column. This pass adds the three real form layouts (standard · fancy · sidebar), the compact record-navigation control, chrome-style flowing tabs, the full Winter.Test field-type gallery, and ≥16px text-entry controls on coarse-pointer (touch) devices so iOS Safari never zooms — on desktop every control shares one ~34px height.
