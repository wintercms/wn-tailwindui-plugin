Headline near-duplicate wins:
  • Silver cluster: 12 hexes / 33 uses (#bdc3c7 #b5babd #bbb #b2b9be…) all mean “placeholder / disabled control” → one token, incl. three identical @color-scroll-indicator vars defined in three files.
  • Off-whites: 10 hexes / 37 uses (#f9f9f9 #f5f5f5 #f6f6f6 #eee #fafafa #f0f0f0…) collapse to just three roles: page bg, hover surface, muted control fill.
  • #2b3e50 vs #2a3e51: the same slate written two ways, 16 uses combined — plus #feffff, which is white with a typo.
  • Nine stray blues re-implement “selected/interactive” that var(--brand-secondary) already owns; seven one-shot oranges do one job (#ec8017 tab marker anchors it).
  • #666 beats #555 17:9 for secondary text even though the named @gray is #555 — standardise on #666.
